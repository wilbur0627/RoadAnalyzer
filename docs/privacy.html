Privacy Policy for RoadAnalyzer

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Introduction

RoadAnalyzer ("we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that analyzes baccarat road patterns for entertainment purposes. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension handles, where it is sent, and why.

Road Images Sent to Our Analysis Server

RoadAnalyzer reads game results from an image of the road chart. This only starts after you click "Select Region" in the extension and drag a box around the road chart on a page.

  • What is sent: a PNG image of the area you selected, and nothing else. To cut that area out, the extension takes a screenshot of the visible tab, but the rest of the screenshot never leaves your device.
  • When it is sent: once right after you select the area, and then every 10 seconds while the tab you selected it on is the active tab in its window. An image is only sent when the selected area has changed since the last one.
  • Where it goes: our analysis server at road-analyzer-henna.vercel.app, hosted on Vercel. The server reads the results from the image, sends them back to the extension, and does not save the image.
  • How it stops: capturing ends when you close that tab or close the browser.

License Validation

When you activate a license key, the extension sends it to LemonSqueezy's License API (api.lemonsqueezy.com), together with the name "RoadAnalyzer Chrome Extension" to label the activation. After that, it checks the key with LemonSqueezy at most about once a day, sending the license key and the activation ID, to confirm that your subscription is still active.

Information Stored on Your Device

The extension keeps the following in Chrome's built-in extension storage:

  • Settings and license (chrome.storage.sync): your language preference, whether you accepted the disclaimer, the position and size of the road area you selected, your plan (Free or Premium), your license key, its activation ID, and when the license was last checked. If Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome syncs these to your other Chrome browsers.
  • Current session (chrome.storage.session): the results of the last analysis, the hands recorded since capturing started (so hands that scroll off the road chart are still counted), a fingerprint (hash) of the last image sent so unchanged images are not sent again, and which tab is being captured. Chrome clears these when you close the browser.

Removing the extension deletes this data from your browser.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your browsing history.
  • We do not track your activity across websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information through the extension.
  • The extension does not show advertisements, use analytics, or set cookies.
  • We do not sell any data.

Third-Party Services

  • LemonSqueezy: our payment processor. When you buy a subscription, your payment details are handled by LemonSqueezy on their checkout page, not by the extension. See LemonSqueezy's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: hosts our analysis server and processes the road images described above on our behalf. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.
